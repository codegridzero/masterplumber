Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our plumbing services? Find quick answers below regarding emergency responses, leak repairs, and routine maintenance.
What should I do first in a plumbing emergency?
Locate and shut off the main water valve immediately to stop the flow of water and prevent structural damage. Once the valve is closed, call our 24/7 emergency dispatch line for a technician to be sent out immediately.
How quickly can your plumbers respond to emergency calls?
For active emergencies (such as burst pipes or severe clogs), we guarantee a response and dispatch time of 30 minutes or less to ensure your home remains safe.
What are the most common signs of a hidden water leak?
Common warning signs include an unexplained increase in your water bill, damp or discolored spots on drywall/ceilings, musty mildew odors, or hearing running water when all plumbing appliances are switched off.
How can I prevent my drain lines from getting clogged?
Avoid pouring cooking oils or fats down kitchen sinks, use hair catches in shower drains, and only flush toilet paper. Regular hot-water rinses can also help clear minor residue buildup.
Why is my water heater making a loud knocking noise?
Knocking or popping noises usually point to sediment buildup at the base of the tank. Flushing the water heater annually removes this sediment, improves heating efficiency, and prolongs the lifespan of your unit.
