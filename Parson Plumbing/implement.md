# Parson Plumbing & Drains — Master Template Exact-Count Content

## Purpose

Paste-ready replacement copy for the existing ProPlumbers master template.
Every field below preserves the master audit's exact declared word count and character count.

## Important implementation rules

- Do not change the template structure, field order, CSS, layout, or component count.
- Replace only the text value for each matching field.
- The audit count convention is preserved: whitespace-separated words and characters including spaces.
- Where necessary, extra regular spaces are intentionally used to reach the exact character count without changing the visible wording in normal HTML rendering. Do not trim/normalize whitespace if exact source-string counts are required.
- Pricing is not published on Parson Plumbing's website, so the pricing section uses free-quote / N/A language instead of inventing dollar prices.
- The website shows one Columbus office and a Central Ohio service area; map pins represent service areas, not separate branch addresses.
- Review copy is paraphrased rather than presented as invented direct quotations. Review cards using shortened/anonymized labels should be replaced with verified full attribution before a live production launch.
- Accreditation alt text is kept generic where the website does not verify the master template's specific association badges.

## Business research basis

Primary source: Parson Plumbing & Drains official website, including the homepage, About page, Services page, service-area page, and individual service pages.
Key verified facts used: Columbus/Central Ohio service area; 24/7 emergency plumbing; locally owned/operated; Dustin Parson founded the company in 2019; State Plumbing Contractor's License; licensed/bonded/insured team; free upfront quotes; no high-pressure sales, sales commissions, or price gouging; residential and commercial services; published service list; Columbus office/contact details.
